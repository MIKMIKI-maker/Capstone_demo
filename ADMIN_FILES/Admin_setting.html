School Year

School Years

Only one School Year is Active at a time. New activities and each teacher's Learning Activity Plan go to the Active School Year, and it has its own Grading Period locks. Past School Years stay viewable as read-only history: teachers pick one from the School Year dropdown on their pages, and you can browse past classes below.

Grading Periods

Learning Activity Plan Access

Controls which grading period teachers can currently add or edit focus areas for, on their Dashboard's Learning Activity Plan. This applies school-wide — unlocking a grading period opens it for every teacher. These locks belong to the Active School Year; a new School Year starts with only First Grading unlocked.
